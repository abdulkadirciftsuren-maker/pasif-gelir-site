If you're shopping for a super-automatic espresso machine, you'll almost certainly end up looking at three brands: **De'Longhi**, **Philips** or **Siemens**. All three make good machines, but their strengths lie in different places. In this comparison we look at the **milk system**, **cleaning**, **grinder**, **ease of use** and **price**.

> **Short answer:** If you mostly drink **black coffee** on a limited budget, get a **De'Longhi Magnifica**. Daily **cappuccino or latte** with minimal cleaning: **Philips LatteGo**. For **families**, **lots of drinks** and **comfort**: the **Siemens EQ** series. With a bigger budget, the De'Longhi Dinamica Plus and Eletta Explore are strong options too.

*Note: Prices are rough European ranges and change a lot during sales. Siemens super-automatics are sold mainly in Europe and the UK; in the US, De'Longhi, Philips and Jura are the most common choices.*

## Quick comparison

| | **De'Longhi** | **Philips** | **Siemens** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strength** | Espresso taste, huge model range | Easy milk cleaning, value for money | Comfort, many drinks, premium feel |
| **Milk system** | Steam wand on entry models, LatteCrema carafe higher up | LatteGo: tube-free, two parts | Tube that draws from a container or carton |
| **Milk cleaning** | Carafe is dishwasher-safe | Easiest: a few seconds under the tap | Automatic steam rinse, tube washed by hand |
| **Brew unit** | Removable | Removable | Removable on most models |
| **Grinder** | Steel conical | Ceramic flat | Ceramic |
| **Controls** | Buttons on entry models, touch higher up | Simple icon touch panel | Clear display, profiles, app (high-end) |
| **Price (approx.)** | €300–1,500 | €350–800 | €400–1,600 |

## De'Longhi: for espresso lovers

The Italian brand has the widest range. The **Magnifica S / Magnifica Evo** have been among the best-selling entry models for years: simple, robust and with good espresso for the money.

- **Pros:** Strong, balanced espresso. Removable brew unit. Spare parts and service are easy to find.
- **Cons:** Entry models froth milk **by hand with a steam wand**. The LatteCrema carafe on higher models makes good foam, but there are more parts to wash.
- **Models:** Magnifica S / Evo (entry), Dinamica Plus (carafe, touch screen), Eletta Explore (hot and cold drinks), PrimaDonna (high-end).

## Philips: the easiest milk cleaning

Philips' **LatteGo** milk system is one of the easiest to clean in this class: no tube, just two plastic parts that you rinse under the tap or put in the dishwasher.

- **Pros:** Milk drinks with very little effort. The **AquaClean** filter means less frequent descaling. Great value.
- **Cons:** The milk foam isn't very fine; it's a bit coarse and dense. Lots of plastic. Fewer coffee settings than the competition.
- **Models:** 2200 / 3200 LatteGo (entry and mid), 5400 LatteGo (more drinks, user profiles).

## Siemens: comfort and variety

Siemens' **EQ** series is aimed at people who want **comfort** and **variety**. (Bosch super-automatics come from the same group, BSH.)

- **Pros:** Lots of drinks, user profiles, two cups at once (with milk too, on most models). Quiet ceramic grinder. Clear display and solid build.
- **Cons:** Higher price. Milk is drawn through a **tube** that needs regular cleaning. Entry models (EQ.3, EQ.300) offer less than rivals.
- **Models:** EQ.300 / EQ.3 (entry), EQ.500 / EQ.6 plus (mid, popular with families), EQ.700 / EQ.900 (high-end, some with two bean hoppers).

## Which one suits you?

- **"I drink 2–3 espressos or black coffees a day."** → De'Longhi Magnifica S / Evo.
- **"Cappuccino every morning, but I hate cleaning."** → Philips 3200 LatteGo.
- **"Everyone at home drinks something different, and we have guests a lot."** → Siemens EQ.6 plus or EQ.700.
- **"I want great milk foam and a touch screen."** → De'Longhi Dinamica Plus.
- **"I want cold coffee drinks too."** → De'Longhi Eletta Explore.

## Other brands?

- **Jura (Switzerland):** Premium, elegant design and excellent milk foam. The brew unit is **not removable**; maintenance runs on cleaning tablets. Clearly more expensive, but widely available in the US.
- **Melitta (Germany):** The Barista T / TS series offers lots of drinks for a fair price and a removable brew unit; common in Europe.

## Whatever you buy: 3 tips

1. **Use fresh beans:** Taste comes more from the beans than the machine. See [how to buy coffee beans](/en/how-to-buy-coffee-beans/).

3. **Watch the price:** These machines often drop a lot during sales. A few weeks of price watching can save $100 or more.

## FAQ

**Which brand lasts the longest?** All three last **many years** with good care. **Scale** and **cleaning** matter most. A removable brew unit makes care easier.

**LatteGo, carafe or tube?** **LatteGo** is the easiest to clean. The best foam usually comes from **carafes** (De'Longhi LatteCrema) and **tube systems** (Siemens). Drawing milk straight from the carton is handy, but rinse the tube daily.

**Do I need a model with an app?** For most people, no. In daily use, a good display and clear buttons matter more.

**Can I buy Siemens in the US?** Siemens super-automatics are rarely sold in the US. Jura and the higher De'Longhi models are the usual premium alternatives there.
