You want to make good coffee at home but don't know where to start? There are hundreds of products – and you don't need to buy everything at once. Here are **3 ready-made starter kits by budget** plus a **bonus Turkish coffee kit**. Each item links to our detailed guide.

> **Short answer:** **Small budget:** a moka pot and a handheld milk frother. **Medium budget:** a V60 kit, a hand grinder and a scale. **Comfort:** a super-automatic machine (or an espresso machine with a grinder). Whatever kit you choose, **fresh beans** are the most important "accessory".

## First, ask yourself: how do you drink coffee?

| Mostly… | Suggested kit |
| --- | --- |
| Milk drinks, small budget | Kit 1: Moka pot kit |
| Black, aromatic pour-over | Kit 2: V60 kit |
| Lots of cups daily, no fuss | Kit 3: Super-automatic kit |
| Turkish coffee | Bonus: Turkish coffee kit |

## Kit 1: Small budget – moka pot kit

**Includes:**

- **Moka pot (3-cup):** espresso-style coffee with no power – see the [moka pot buying guide](/en/moka-pot-buying-guide/) and [how to use it](/en/how-to-use-a-moka-pot/).
- **Handheld milk frother:** for lattes and cappuccinos – see the [milk frother guide](/en/milk-frother-guide/).
- **Medium-dark beans or coffee ground for a moka pot.**

**Who it's for:** Milk-drink fans, students, small kitchens.

**Next step:** add a hand grinder.

## Kit 2: Medium budget – V60 and grinder kit

**Includes:**

- **V60 starter kit:** dripper, filters and server – see the [V60 guide](/en/v60-pour-over-guide/).
- **Hand grinder:** the piece that makes the biggest difference – see the [grinder guide](/en/best-coffee-grinder/).
- **Scale with timer:** the same taste every day – see the [coffee scale guide](/en/coffee-scale-guide/).
- **Optional gooseneck kettle:** for controlled pouring – see the [gooseneck kettle guide](/en/gooseneck-kettle-guide/).
- **Fresh light-to-medium roast beans** – see [what is specialty coffee](/en/what-is-specialty-coffee/).

**Who it's for:** Black coffee drinkers who want to explore flavors.

**Alternative:** Instead of a V60, an [AeroPress](/en/how-to-use-an-aeropress/) (easier, more forgiving) or a [French press](/en/how-to-use-a-french-press/) (more body).

## Kit 3: Comfort – super-automatic kit

**Includes:**

- **Super-automatic espresso machine:** grinds the beans itself; espresso and milk drinks at the push of a button – see the [super-automatic guide](/en/super-automatic-espresso-machine-guide/).
- **Water filter and descaler:** protect the machine – see [how to descale a coffee maker](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/).
- **Medium roast beans.**

**Who it's for:** Families who want several cups a day without effort.

**Alternative:** For hobby baristas, an [espresso machine with a grinder](/en/home-espresso-machine-guide/). The most convenient but priciest per cup: a [capsule machine](/en/capsule-coffee-machine-guide/).

## Bonus: Turkish coffee kit

- **Cezve:** suited to your stove, 1–2 cups – see [how to choose a cezve](/en/how-to-choose-a-cezve/).
- **Turkish coffee cup set.**
- **Fresh Turkish coffee** in small packs – see [how to buy Turkish coffee](/en/how-to-buy-turkish-coffee/).
- **Optional Turkish coffee machine** – see the [Turkish coffee machine guide](/en/turkish-coffee-machine-guide/).

## Don't forget, whatever kit you choose

1. **Fresh coffee:** Even the best gear can't save stale coffee – see [how to tell if coffee is fresh](/en/how-to-tell-if-coffee-is-fresh/).
2. **Good water:** see [best water for coffee](/en/best-water-for-coffee/).
3. **Proper storage:** see [how to store coffee beans](/en/how-to-store-coffee-beans/).
4. **Step by step:** Start with the basics and add pieces as you need them.

## FAQ

**What should I buy first?** Usually a **grinder**. Freshly ground coffee improves taste the most, whatever your brew method.

**Which kit makes a good gift?** Kit 1 and the Turkish coffee kit are great gifts. More ideas in [coffee gift ideas](/en/coffee-gift-ideas/).

**My coffee tastes bitter or sour – is my gear wrong?** It's usually the settings – see [why is my coffee bitter or sour?](/en/why-is-my-coffee-bitter/).
