Along with chocolate, **coffee advent calendars** have become a holiday favorite: behind each door is **a cup of a different coffee**, from December 1 to 24. This guide covers **the types**, **what to check before buying** and **how to make your own**.

> **Short answer:** Choose a calendar that matches **how you brew**: beans, ground, pods or drip bags. Check the **grams per door** and the **roast date**. Popular calendars sell out by **mid-November**, so buy early. For a cheaper, more personal gift, **fill 24 small bags yourself**.

## When does it start?

Most calendars run from **December 1 to 24**. (In 2026, the first Sunday of Advent is November 29.) As a gift, it should arrive **by the end of November**.

## The types

| Type | Behind each door | Best for | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Whole bean** | 10–20 g of beans | Grinder owners | You'll need a grinder; see [best coffee grinder](/en/best-coffee-grinder/) |
| **Ground** | 10–20 g, filter or espresso grind | No grinder | Does the grind match your brewer? |
| **Pods** | 1–2 pods | Pod machine owners | Check the **system** (Nespresso Original, Vertuo, Keurig) |
| **Drip bags** | 1 pour-over bag | Everyone, no machine needed | The easiest gift |
| **Coffee + extras** | Coffee, syrup, chocolate, small gadgets | Gift lovers | Often not much coffee |

The most common mistake: **a pod calendar for the wrong system.** Nespresso Original and Vertuo pods aren't interchangeable. See the [capsule coffee machine guide](/en/capsule-coffee-machine-guide/).

## What to check before buying

1. **Amount per door:** 10 g is usually a small cup; 15–20 g makes a mug or two espressos. Check the total.
2. **Roast date:** The coffee waits for weeks. Small packs with a **valve or nitrogen flush** and a roast date stay fresh. See [how to tell if coffee is fresh](/en/how-to-tell-if-coffee-is-fresh/).
3. **Variety:** Good calendars mix origins and roast levels; a **tasting card** is a plus. See [coffee growing regions](/en/coffee-growing-regions/).
4. **Price per ounce:** Calendars usually cost **more per gram** than regular coffee, since you pay for the box and packaging. Fine for a gift; do the math for yourself.
5. **Packaging:** Lots of plastic or paper? Some roasters use recyclable bags.
6. **Buy early:** Small-roaster calendars are limited and **sell out in November**.

## Make your own coffee advent calendar

Cheaper, more personal and tailored to the person you're giving it to.

**You'll need:**

- **24 small bags or envelopes** (kraft paper, zip bags or mini jars)
- **3–6 different coffees** (beans or ground) or a mixed **drip bag** sampler
- Number stickers or a pen, string or a box
- Optional: mini syrups, chocolate, cinnamon sticks, small recipe cards

**Steps:**

1. **Portion the coffee:** **15 g** per bag (one mug). A [coffee scale](/en/coffee-scale-guide/) makes it easy.
2. **Plan the order:** Light to dark, or one country per week (e.g., week 1 Ethiopia, week 2 Colombia).
3. **Add small surprises:** Chocolate on day 6, a mini syrup on day 13 (see [coffee syrup guide](/en/coffee-syrup-guide/)), Turkish coffee with Turkish delight on another day.
4. **Recipe cards:** Add a short recipe on a few days: [winter coffee drinks](/en/winter-coffee-drinks/), [chai latte](/en/chai-latte-recipe/) or [hot chocolate](/en/hot-chocolate-recipe/).
5. **Keep it fresh:** Seal the bags airtight and store the calendar somewhere cool and dark. See [how to store coffee beans](/en/how-to-store-coffee-beans/).
6. **Number and hang:** Clip them to a string or line them up in a box.

**Tip:** Pack ground coffee **no more than 1–2 weeks** ahead; it loses aroma fast. Whole beans keep longer.

## Getting the best from your calendar

- **A method for small doses:** For 10–20 g, an [AeroPress](/en/how-to-use-an-aeropress/) or [V60](/en/v60-pour-over-guide/) is ideal.
- **Take notes:** Write down each coffee and how you liked it. By Christmas you'll know which origins and roasts you love. See [coffee roast levels](/en/coffee-roast-levels/).
- **Buy your favorite:** The calendar is a perfect tasting flight for your next bag.

## FAQ

**Are coffee advent calendars for kids?** No, they're for adults. For kids, a chocolate calendar or [hot chocolate](/en/hot-chocolate-recipe/) ingredients work better.

**Are there decaf calendars?** Some roasters offer them or mix in decaf days. If you make your own, use [decaf coffee](/en/decaf-coffee-guide/) for evening days.

**When should I buy?** Ideally **early to mid-November**. After that, many are sold out.
