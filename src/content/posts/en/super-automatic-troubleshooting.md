A super-automatic gives you coffee at the push of a button, until one day it only drips, the milk won't froth or a warning keeps flashing. The good news: most problems can be fixed **at home in a few minutes, without a service call**. Here are the **10 most common problems**, their **causes** and **fixes**.

> **Short answer:** Most problems come down to three things: a **dirty brew unit**, **scale** and the **wrong grind setting**. First **rinse the brew unit**, run the **cleaning and descaling programs** and change the grind **by one step**. For milk problems, **take the milk system apart and clean it**. If that doesn't help, call service.

⚠️ **Safety:** Switch the machine off and **unplug it** before taking anything apart. Only remove parts the manual describes; opening the housing can void the warranty.

## Quick table: symptom and first step

| Symptom | Likely cause | Try first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No coffee / only drips | Grind too fine, brew unit clogged, scale | Grind coarser, rinse the brew unit |
| Watery coffee | Grind too coarse, beans not feeding | Grind finer, raise the dose |
| Lukewarm coffee | Cold cup, scale | Preheat the cup, descale |
| Milk won't froth | Dirty milk system, warm milk | Take the milk system apart, use cold milk |
| Dripping / leaking | Full drip tray, seal | Empty the tray, reseat the water tank |
| Brew unit stuck | Wrong position | Switch off normally, realign |
| Grinder noise | Foreign object, oily beans | Empty the hopper, check |
| Descale warning won't clear | Program interrupted | Run the full program |

## 1. No coffee, or only drips

- **Grind too fine:** Water can't get through. Go **1–2 steps coarser**. On many machines (e.g., De'Longhi) you should adjust **only while the grinder is running**; check the manual.
- **Clogged brew unit:** Remove it, rinse **with lukewarm water only** (no soap), let it dry and put it back. Ideally weekly.
- **Scale:** The pump and lines are narrowed. Run the [descaling program](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/).
- **Water tank / air:** Remove the tank and seat it firmly; on some machines running a little hot water clears the air.

## 2. Coffee is watery and weak

- **Grind too coarse:** One step finer, then taste after **3–4 cups** (the change isn't instant).
- **Dose too low:** Raise the strength setting.
- **Beans not feeding:** Very **oily, dark** beans stick in the hopper. Stir them and choose less oily, medium roasts. See [how to buy coffee beans](/en/how-to-buy-coffee-beans/).
- **Pre-ground chute:** Did you select ground-coffee mode by mistake?

## 3. Coffee is lukewarm

- **Preheat the cup:** A cold cup cools coffee instantly. Run hot water first or use the cup warmer.
- **Temperature setting:** Set it to "high" in the menu.
- **Scale:** It insulates the heater. Descale and set the water hardness correctly. See [best water for coffee](/en/best-water-for-coffee/).
- **First cup:** After a long break, the first cup is cooler; run a rinse first.

## 4. Milk won't froth

- **Is the milk cold?** Straight from the fridge froths best.
- **Type of milk:** Whole or 2% froths well. For plant milks, choose a **barista** version. See [oat milk for coffee](/en/oat-milk-for-coffee/).
- **Dirty milk system:** Dried milk blocks the air intake. **Take apart** the milk container and tube, rinse in warm water and use a **milk system cleaner** weekly.
- **Assembled wrong:** If a part is reversed or missing, the system won't draw air; check the picture in the manual.

## 5. Dripping or leaking

- **Drip tray and grounds container:** Probably full; empty them.
- **Water tank:** Not seated properly or the seal is dirty. Remove, wipe the seal, reseat.
- **Brew unit seals:** Dried out or worn. Lightly grease the moving parts with **food-grade silicone grease** (if the manual allows).
- **A little water is normal:** Rinsing sends water into the tray.

## 6. Brew unit won't come out or go back in

- **Don't force it!** If the machine wasn't switched off normally, the unit stays in the wrong position. Turn the machine on, **switch it off normally**, then try again.
- **Alignment:** Some units have an arrow or mark; set it to the position shown in the manual.
- **Dry and stiff:** Lightly grease with food-grade grease.

## 7. Grinder makes noise or won't grind

- **Foreign object:** A small stone can hide among the beans. Switch off, unplug, empty the hopper and check. If the noise stays, call service.
- **Oily beans:** They slowly gum up the grinder; switch to less oily beans.
- **Never** put water or rice in the grinder; use special grinder cleaners only if the maker approves them.

## 8. Descale warning won't go away

- Run the program **all the way through**; if interrupted, the warning stays.
- Set the **water hardness** correctly in the machine (a test strip usually comes in the box).
- If you use a water filter, **activate it in the menu**, or the machine will miscalculate.

## 9. Coffee tastes bitter or sour

Usually not a fault but a settings issue: grind, roast level, a dirty brew unit. Full guide: [why is my coffee bitter or sour?](/en/why-is-my-coffee-bitter/)

## 10. Machine won't turn on or shows an error code

- **Unplug, wait 1 minute**, plug back in. Many temporary errors clear this way.
- Look up the **error code** in the manual or on the maker's website.
- Check that all doors and containers are in place (some sensors block with a door open).

## When to call service

- The pump runs but **no water** comes out, even after descaling.
- Water leaks **inside** the machine.
- A **burning smell**, sparks or a tripped breaker.
- The grinder stays noisy after you've removed a stone.

Still under warranty? Contact the retailer or manufacturer before taking anything apart.

## Prevent problems

- **Daily:** Empty the grounds container and drip tray, rinse the milk system.
- **Weekly:** Remove and rinse the brew unit.
- **Monthly or when prompted:** Cleaning tablet and descaling. The full schedule: [how to clean a coffee maker](/en/how-to-clean-a-coffee-maker/).
- **Right beans:** Medium rather than very oily roasts. See [coffee roast levels](/en/coffee-roast-levels/).

## FAQ

**Can I wash the brew unit with dish soap?** Usually not; lukewarm water only. Soap attacks seals and lubricant. Check the manual.

**Can I descale with vinegar?** Not recommended for super-automatics; vinegar can damage seals and may affect the warranty. Use the descaler the maker recommends.

**How often should I change the grind?** When you switch beans or the taste changes. One step at a time, and judge after a few cups.
