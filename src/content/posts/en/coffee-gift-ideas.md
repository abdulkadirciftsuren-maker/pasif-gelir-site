Coffee is always a safe bet – but what gift will really delight a coffee lover? The good news: the best coffee gifts often aren't expensive at all. Here are **15 ideas, sorted by budget**, with quick tips on who they suit.

> **Short answer:** If you're not sure: **fresh beans from a roaster** (under $25), a **moka pot** or a **V60 kit** ($25–50), or a **hand grinder** ($50–100). Before giving an expensive machine, find out how the person actually drinks their coffee.

## First, think: how do they drink their coffee?

| They drink … | Good gifts |
| --- | --- |
| Black coffee from a drip machine | Fresh beans, hand grinder, V60 kit |
| Cappuccino and latte | Milk frother, nice cups, beans |
| Espresso | Moka pot, coffee scale, espresso machine |
| Turkish coffee | Cezve, small Turkish coffee cups, fresh Turkish coffee |
| Capsules | A first step into freshly ground coffee: grinder + French press |

## Under $25

1. **Fresh beans from a roaster:** Always welcome. Check the roast date – tips in [How to buy coffee beans](/en/how-to-buy-coffee-beans/).
2. **Cezve:** Small, beautiful and unusual. Which one to choose: [How to choose a cezve](/en/how-to-choose-a-cezve/).
3. **Handheld milk frother:** Milk foam without a big machine – see the [milk frother guide](/en/milk-frother-guide/).
4. **Coffee scale with timer:** Sounds technical, but makes every coffee better – see the [coffee scale guide](/en/coffee-scale-guide/).
5. **Double-walled glasses or nice cups:** For espresso, cappuccino or latte.

## $25–50

6. **Moka pot:** The Italian classic. How to use it: [How to use a moka pot](/en/how-to-use-a-moka-pot/).
7. **V60 kit:** Dripper, filter papers and server – the easiest way into specialty coffee. Guide: [V60 pour-over guide](/en/v60-pour-over-guide/).
8. **French press:** Uncomplicated, sturdy and good for several cups – see [How to use a French press](/en/how-to-use-a-french-press/).
9. **Turkish coffee set:** A cezve, two small cups and freshly ground Turkish coffee – see [How to buy Turkish coffee](/en/how-to-buy-turkish-coffee/).

## $50–100

10. **Hand grinder:** The biggest jump in flavor for anyone who buys pre-ground. More in [Best coffee grinder](/en/best-coffee-grinder/).
11. **Electric milk frother:** Hot and cold foam at the touch of a button.
12. **Gooseneck kettle:** The favorite tool of every pour-over fan – see the [gooseneck kettle guide](/en/gooseneck-kettle-guide/).

## Over $100 – the big gift

13. **A good drip coffee maker:** For anyone who drinks a full pot every day – see the [drip coffee maker guide](/en/drip-coffee-maker-guide/).
14. **Espresso machine:** For home baristas, ideally together with a grinder – see the [home espresso machine guide](/en/home-espresso-machine-guide/).
15. **Super-automatic espresso machine:** For anyone who loves convenience – see the [super-automatic guide](/en/super-automatic-espresso-machine-guide/).

## 4 tips for the perfect coffee gift

1. **Don't surprise people with big machines:** Machines depend heavily on taste and counter space. Better to ask discreetly first.
2. **Beans, not ground coffee:** Anyone with a grinder will be happy with whole beans. Without a grinder, give a small amount of freshly ground coffee.
3. **Combine:** Small gifts feel bigger as a set – e.g. cezve + cups + coffee, or V60 + filters + beans.
4. **Order early:** Before the holidays, popular models sell out and delivery times get longer.

## FAQ

**What do you give someone who has everything?** Fresh beans from a special roaster or an unusual origin, such as Ethiopia. Coffee gets used up – it will always be put to use.

**Is a capsule machine a good gift?** It's convenient, but the capsules are expensive per cup. True coffee lovers are usually happier with a grinder or a pour-over dripper. More in the [capsule coffee machine guide](/en/capsule-coffee-machine-guide/).

**What's an especially personal coffee gift?** A Turkish coffee set with a short guide to reading coffee grounds. More on how to make it in [How to make Turkish coffee](/en/how-to-make-turkish-coffee/).
