A Turkish coffee machine handles the hardest part of using a cezve: **catching the moment the foam rises.** You add coffee, water and sugar, press a button, and the machine heats slowly and stops when the foam is up. Which machine is right depends entirely on how you'll use it.

> **Short answer:** Alone or for two, a **compact machine** is enough. Lots of guests? Look at **multi-cup models with a water tank**. On a small budget or without a stove, start with an **electric cezve**.

## Machine or cezve?

| | Turkish coffee machine | Cezve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foam | Similar every time, reliable | Depends on skill; best in experienced hands |
| Effort | Press a button and wait | Stay by the stove |
| Boiling over | Very unlikely | High if you're not watching |
| Many guests | Easy with multi-cup models | You need several cezves |
| Price | Higher | Very low |
| Lifespan | Electronics can fail | A lifetime |

To learn the cezve method, see [How to make Turkish coffee](/en/how-to-make-turkish-coffee/).

## 7 things to check before buying

1. **Capacity:** Compact models make 1–2 cups at a time. For frequent guests, look at 3–4 cup models.
2. **Water tank or not:** Some machines take water straight into the brewing pot; others measure it from a tank. Tank models are more convenient but need regular descaling.
3. **Foam control:** A good machine stops heating when the foam rises. An audible alert helps on busy mornings.
4. **Milk mode:** If you like Turkish coffee with milk, check for it specifically – milk boils over faster and not every machine handles it.
5. **Easy cleaning:** Can the brewing pot be removed? Coffee residue causes bad smells and taste over time.
6. **Warranty, service, plug:** For imported machines, check the plug type, voltage and that the warranty is valid in your country.
7. **Size and design:** It will live on your counter – check the dimensions.

## Which machine for whom?

| Situation | Recommended type |

## 5 tips for more foam

1. **Use cold water.** Warm water makes the coffee cook before foam forms.
2. **Use fresh coffee.** Opened coffee loses its foaming power within weeks.
3. **Measure consistently.** Use the machine's spoon and cup.
4. **Stir as the manual says.** Some machines want the ingredients mixed first, others don't.
5. **Keep the pot clean.** Oil and grounds from last time ruin the foam.

## Care

- Rinse and dry the brewing pot **after every use**.
- Clean the inside with a soft sponge **once a week** – no abrasives.
- **Tank models** build up limescale, which slows heating and spoils the taste. Descale as the manufacturer recommends – more often if your water is hard.
- **Never immerse** the machine in water.

## FAQ

**Does a machine make coffee as good as a cezve?** With fresh coffee and the right amount, yes – very good. Experienced hands still get the best foam from a cezve, but the machine gives a good result every time with no effort.

**Do I need special coffee?** No. Any Turkish coffee ground as fine as powder works. Freshness matters most.
