If you can buy only **one** piece of gear to improve your coffee at home, make it a good grinder. An expensive machine cannot rescue badly ground coffee, while a good grinder can make a great cup even from a basic French press.

## Why the grinder matters so much

Coffee starts losing aroma the moment it is ground. Brewing is water extracting flavor from particles; if those particles are uneven, the small ones over-extract and the big ones under-extract, and you get bitterness and sourness in the same cup.

## Blade or burr?

- **Blade grinders** smash beans like a propeller. Cheap, but they produce a mix of dust and boulders.
- **Burr grinders** crush beans between two burrs, producing even particles, and are adjustable for every method.

> **Short answer:** Whatever your budget, get a burr grinder. An affordable manual burr grinder beats an expensive blade grinder.

## Manual or electric?

| | Manual | Electric |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grind quality at the same price | Higher | Lower |
| Speed and effort | 30–60 s of cranking | One button |
| Noise | Quiet | Loud |
| Portability | Travel friendly | Lives on the counter |

For one or two cups of filter coffee a day, a manual grinder is unbeatable value. If the whole household drinks coffee or mornings are rushed, go electric.

## Which grind for which method?

- **Turkish coffee:** Powder fine. Not every grinder can go this fine — check the specs.
- **Espresso:** Fine, with very precise adjustment.
- **V60 / pour-over:** Medium-fine, like sand.
- **French press / cold brew:** Coarse, like sea salt.

For a detailed recipe, see our [V60 guide](/en/v60-pour-over-guide/).

**Looking for a Turkish coffee grinder?** Brass mills and hand grinders with a Turkish setting: [Turkish coffee grinder](/en/turkish-coffee-grinder/).

## Checklist before you buy

1. Are the burrs stainless steel or ceramic? (Steel is usually sharper and more consistent.)
2. Does it have enough range for your brew method?
3. Can it be taken apart easily for cleaning?
4. Are spare parts and service available where you live?
