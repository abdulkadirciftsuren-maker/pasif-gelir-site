November is the busiest month of the year for buying a coffee maker: **Black Friday**, the week of deals around it and **Cyber Monday**. Some machines drop a lot; other "deals" are just an inflated price brought back down. This guide covers **when**, **which machines** and **how to spot a real discount**.

> **Short answer:** First decide **which type of machine** you want and shortlist **2–3 models**. Set up **price tracking** now (camelcamelcamel or Keepa for Amazon). When the sale comes, compare the price with the **lowest price of the last 2–3 months**, not the crossed-out "list price". Check the **seller**, **warranty** and **return window**.

## When is it in 2026?

- **Thanksgiving:** November 26, 2026. **Black Friday:** November 27. **Cyber Monday:** November 30.
- **Amazon and big retailers** usually start "Black Friday week" deals 1–2 weeks early.

**Tip:** The best price isn't always on Friday. Some machines drop a week before, others again on Cyber Monday. That's why price alerts help.

## Which machines drop the most?

| Type | On sale | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Super-automatic** (De'Longhi, Philips, Jura entry models) | Often and deeply | Entry and mid-range models drop the most |
| **Capsule machines** (Nespresso, Keurig) | Very cheap, often with pod bundles | The real cost is the pods, not the machine |
| **Home espresso machines** (Breville, De'Longhi) | Moderate discounts | Budget for a grinder too |
| **Milk frothers, grinders, scales** | Often | A good time for gifts |
| **Premium brands** (Moccamaster, high-end espresso) | Rarely and modestly | Buy when you need it rather than wait |

Not sure which type suits you? Take the [coffee maker quiz](/en/coffee-maker-quiz/) or read [best coffee maker](/en/best-coffee-maker/).

## How to spot a real discount

1. **Check the price history:** **camelcamelcamel** and **Keepa** show Amazon price charts; Google Shopping compares retailers.
2. **Don't trust the "list price":** The crossed-out price is often one nobody ever paid. What counts is the **lowest price of the last 2–3 months**.
3. **Compare model numbers:** The same machine comes in color or accessory variants with different numbers, and retailer-exclusive models exist. Sometimes the "deal" is an older or lesser model.
4. **Price out bundles:** For "with pods" or "with milk carafe" deals, subtract the real value of the extra, then compare.
5. **Think total cost:** Even at $50, a capsule machine's pods soon cost more than the machine. The math: [capsule vs super-automatic](/en/capsule-vs-super-automatic/).

## Checklist before you buy

- [ ] **Seller:** Sold and shipped by Amazon or the retailer itself, or a third-party seller? Check ratings.
- [ ] **Warranty:** Manufacturer warranty valid in your country? Be careful with gray-market imports.
- [ ] **Returns:** Check the return window. Amazon usually extends holiday returns until the end of January, but check first.
- [ ] **Delivery:** Shipping can slow down; for gifts, check the date.
- [ ] **Size and water:** Does it fit under your cabinets? Hard water? Plan for a filter and descaler: [how to descale a coffee maker](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/).
- [ ] **Extra costs:** For espresso machines, a grinder and accessories: [espresso accessories](/en/espresso-accessories/).

## Common mistakes

- **Buying a machine just because it's "deeply discounted."** The wrong type ends up in a cupboard, however cheap.
- **Looking only at percentages.** "50% off" sounds great; the lowest past price is what matters.
- **Last-minute pressure.** "Only 2 left" creates stress. Set your list and target price in advance.
- **Unknown shops.** Avoid ultra-cheap stores with no reviews or contact details.

## Missed the deal?

Black Friday isn't the only chance. Similar prices often show up on **Prime Day** (summer), **after the holidays** and when **a new model** replaces an old one. **Refurbished** or **open-box** machines with a warranty can be a smart buy too.

## FAQ

**Is Black Friday the best time to buy a coffee maker?** Often the year's lowest prices come in November, but not for every model. Don't decide without checking the price history.

**Which brands drop the most?** Usually entry and mid-range super-automatics (De'Longhi, Philips) and capsule machines. Brand comparison: [De'Longhi vs Philips vs Siemens](/en/delonghi-vs-philips-vs-siemens/).

**How big a discount is "good"?** There's no fixed number. If the price matches or beats the lowest of the last 2–3 months, it's a good one.

**Is last year's model worth it?** Often yes. Older models drop a lot when a successor arrives, and the differences are usually small.
