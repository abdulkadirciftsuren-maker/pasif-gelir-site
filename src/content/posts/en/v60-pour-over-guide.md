With its cone shape and single large hole, the V60 leaves flow rate entirely up to you. That makes it one of the least forgiving, and most rewarding, pour-over methods. The good news: follow a few simple rules and you can get a great cup on your first try.

> **Quick recipe:** 15 g coffee, 250 g water (1:16), 93–96 °C, medium-fine grind, 2:30–3:15 total time.

## What you need

- A V60 02 dripper and matching paper filters
- 15 g of freshly roasted coffee (roasted within the last 2–6 weeks is ideal)
- A scale — weighing is far more consistent than using scoops
- A gooseneck kettle if you have one
- A grinder — grinding right before brewing makes the biggest difference

Need amounts for more cups? Use our [coffee ratio calculator](/en/coffee-ratio-calculator/).

## Step by step

1. **Heat the water** to 93–96 °C. No thermometer? Let boiling water rest for 30 seconds.
2. **Rinse the filter** with hot water to remove paper taste and preheat the cup. Discard that water.
3. **Add the coffee.** Put 15 g of ground coffee in the filter, shake gently to level it, tare the scale.
4. **Bloom.** Pour about 30–45 g of water to wet all the grounds and wait 30–45 seconds while CO₂ escapes.
5. **Main pour.** Pour in small circles from the center outward. Reach 150 g by 1:15 and 250 g by 1:45.
6. **Finish.** Give the dripper a gentle swirl to flatten the bed. The brew is done when the water has drained.

## Dial it in by taste

| Taste | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sour, thin, flat | Under-extracted | Grind finer or use slightly hotter water |
| Bitter, dry, harsh | Over-extracted | Grind coarser |
| Watery | Too little coffee | Move toward 1:15 |
| Heavy, too intense | Too much coffee | Move toward 1:17 |

Change **one thing at a time**, otherwise you will not know what made the difference.

## Common mistakes

- **Pre-ground coffee.** Ground coffee starts losing aroma within minutes.
- **Measuring by scoop.** Bean density varies; a scoop can be 5 g or 8 g.
- **Pouring on the paper.** Water runs down the filter without touching the coffee.
- **Boiling water** on dark roasts, which amplifies bitterness.

## Bottom line

The secret to the V60 is consistency: same dose, same water, same timing. Once that is in place, experimenting with different beans is the fun part.
