Good coffee is a small but real morale boost at work. The wrong machine, though, means a queue at 9 a.m., grounds bins that are always full and a milk system nobody cleans. This guide covers the **right machine for your team size**, **cost per cup**, **buying vs leasing** and **hygiene**.

> **Short answer:** Count on **2–3 cups** per person per day. **Up to 5 people**, a good home super-automatic or drip machine is enough. For **5–15 people**, a drip machine with a thermal carafe or a strong home super-automatic. From **15–50 people**, an **office or commercial super-automatic** with a **service contract**. And before choosing a machine, decide **who's responsible for cleaning**.

## First, do the math: how many cups a day?

| Team size | Cups per day (2–3 per person) | Suitable machine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **2–5** | 5–15 | Home super-automatic, drip machine or capsule |
| **5–15** | 10–45 | Drip machine with thermal carafe or a strong home super-automatic |
| **15–30** | 30–90 | Office super-automatic (e.g. Jura WE8, Melitta XT series) |
| **30–60** | 60–180 | Commercial super-automatic (e.g. WMF, Franke, Jura X series), plumbed in |
| **60+** | 150+ | Several machines or a coffee corner / barista |

**Tip:** The share of **milk drinks** matters. If most people drink cappuccino, choose a machine with a strong, easy-to-clean milk system.

## Home or office machine?

Home super-automatics usually handle **20–40 cups a day** comfortably. Beyond that:

- water, beans and grounds need refilling and emptying **constantly**,
- the machine wears faster, and **repairs** and **warranty problems** follow (some makers exclude commercial use for home machines).

**Office machines** have bigger containers, stronger grinders and pumps, an option to **plumb into the water line**, sometimes a **payment system** (for coworking spaces) and a **cup counter**.

## Buy, rent or lease?

- **Buying:** Cheapest in the long run; maintenance and breakdown risk are on you.
- **Rent / lease:** A fixed monthly fee, usually **including maintenance and service**. Easy on cash flow.
- **Machine from your coffee supplier:** Some suppliers provide the machine cheaply if you buy **a set amount of coffee**. Check the **minimum purchase, term and exit terms**. More on this model: [coffee supplier for your café](/en/coffee-supplier-for-cafe/).

Always ask about **response time**: how fast does a technician come when it breaks? A day without coffee in the office gets noticed.

## Hygiene: the most neglected topic

- **Milk system:** Clean it daily. Ideally choose a machine with **automatic milk cleaning**.
- **Cleaning rota:** Write down who empties the **grounds bin**, **drip tray** and cleans the **milk system** on which day. "Everyone" means nobody.
- **Weekly:** Rinse the brew unit (if removable) and run a cleaning tablet.
- **Scale:** Use a water filter and descale when the machine asks. See [how to descale a coffee maker](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/) and [best water for coffee](/en/best-water-for-coffee/).
- **Milk:** Keep opened milk in the fridge; some machines have a **milk cooler**.

## Checklist

- [ ] How many cups a day, and how many with milk?
- [ ] Is there **space**, a **power outlet** and, if needed, a **water connection**?
- [ ] Is **noise** an issue in an open-plan office?
- [ ] Are **cleaning duties** and a rota agreed?
- [ ] Is the **response time** written into the service contract?
- [ ] Is there **decaf** and **hot water for tea**?
- [ ] Who handles **beans and milk** supply?
- [ ] **Mugs** instead of disposable cups?

## FAQ

**What's the best machine for a small office?** For 5–10 people, a **drip machine with a thermal carafe** plus a small **milk frother** is cheap and practical. If lots of milk drinks are on the menu, a home super-automatic with a milk carafe.

**Are capsules a good idea for an office?** For a few people and cups, yes: clean and easy. As the team grows, the **cost per cup** climbs fast.

**Is the machine a business expense?** For companies it usually is. Rules vary by country, so check with your accountant.

**Who should clean the machine?** A written **cleaning rota** works best. In larger offices, it can be part of the cleaning or service contract.
