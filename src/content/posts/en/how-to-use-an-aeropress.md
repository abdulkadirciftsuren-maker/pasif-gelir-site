The AeroPress looks like a big plastic syringe, and it's one of the most versatile coffee makers around. Coffee and water steep briefly together, then you press everything through a filter with the plunger. The result: clean, strong and not very bitter coffee. It's shatterproof, light and cleans up in a minute – great at home and on the go.

> **Short answer:** Stir **15 g** of medium-fine coffee with **220 g** of water at about **90 °C (195 °F)**, steep **1.5–2 minutes**, then press slowly over **20–30 seconds**. For something stronger, make a concentrate with **18 g coffee to 90 g water** and top up with hot water or milk.

## What is an AeroPress?

It has three main parts: the **chamber**, the **plunger** and the **filter cap** with a paper filter. Coffee and water steep in the chamber, then the plunger pushes the coffee through the filter with gentle pressure.

- **Like a French press:** the coffee steeps in water for a while.
- **Like pour-over:** the paper filter keeps out the grounds.
- **Espresso-like:** you can make a strong concentrate, but it isn't real espresso – an espresso machine uses much higher pressure.

## What you need

- An AeroPress and paper filters
- 15–18 g of coffee, medium-fine (between table salt and Turkish coffee)
- Water at about 90 °C / 195 °F (wait 30–60 seconds after boiling)
- A mug, ideally a [coffee scale](/en/coffee-scale-guide/)

## Recipe 1: Standard method

1. **Rinse the filter:** Put the paper filter in the cap and wet it with hot water – no papery taste, and the mug warms up.
2. **Assemble:** Screw the cap onto the chamber and set it on the mug.
3. **Add coffee:** 15 g, shake to level.
4. **Add water:** 220 g, stir for 10 seconds.
5. **Insert the plunger:** Set it in lightly – this mostly stops dripping.
6. **Wait:** 1.5–2 minutes in total.
7. **Press:** Slowly and evenly over 20–30 seconds. Stop at the hissing sound.

## Recipe 2: Inverted method

Nothing drips while it steeps, and the taste is closer to a French press.

1. Push the plunger a little way into the chamber and stand the AeroPress **upside down** on the plunger.
2. Add 17 g of medium-ground coffee and 230 g of water, stir.
3. Screw on the cap with a rinsed filter and wait **2 minutes**.
4. Put your mug upside down on top and **carefully flip both together**.
5. Press over 20–30 seconds.

⚠️ Careful when flipping – it's full of hot water. Work on a stable surface.

## Recipe 3: Concentrate for milk drinks and iced coffee

1. 18 g of finely ground coffee, 90 g of water (about 90 °C).
2. Stir for 20 seconds, wait 1 minute, press slowly.
3. **Americano:** Top up with 100–150 ml (3–5 oz) of hot water.
4. **Latte:** Add frothed milk – see the [milk frother guide](/en/milk-frother-guide/) and [latte vs cappuccino vs flat white](/en/latte-vs-cappuccino-vs-flat-white/).
5. **Iced coffee:** Press straight onto a glass full of ice, then add milk or [syrup](/en/coffee-syrup-guide/) if you like.

## Dialing in the taste

| Coffee tastes… | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Bitter | Grind a bit coarser, steep shorter or use slightly cooler water |
| Sour | Grind a bit finer, steep longer or use hotter water |
| Watery | Use more coffee |
| Very hard to press | Grind is too fine – go coarser and press slowly |

## Cleaning

Unscrew the cap, push the plunger all the way and pop the coffee puck and filter into the trash. Rinse the rubber seal and you're done. Don't store it with the plunger pushed in – the seal can lose its shape over time.

## FAQ

**Can you make Turkish coffee with an AeroPress?** No. Turkish coffee is ground as fine as flour and cooked in a cezve – see [how to make Turkish coffee](/en/how-to-make-turkish-coffee/).

**AeroPress, French press or V60?** The AeroPress is the fastest for one cup and the easiest to clean. A French press suits several people, and a V60 brings out clear, fruity flavors – see the [French press](/en/how-to-use-a-french-press/) and [V60](/en/v60-pour-over-guide/) guides.

**Which coffee?** Freshly roasted, medium roast is a good start – see [how to buy coffee beans](/en/how-to-buy-coffee-beans/).
