On a cold evening, nothing beats a mug of hot chocolate. Packet mixes are handy but usually thin and very sweet. Made with **real chocolate**, hot chocolate tastes far better and is ready almost as fast. Here are **4 methods** and the café secrets behind them.

> **Short answer:** Heat 250 ml (1 cup) milk without boiling it. Add **40 g chopped dark chocolate**, **1 tsp cocoa** and **a pinch of salt**, and whisk until melted. Sweeten to taste. The keys to a creamy cup are **real chocolate** and **whisking**.

## Which chocolate?

- **Dark (55–70% cacao):** the most balanced, fullest flavor. Our first choice.
- **Milk chocolate:** sweeter and milder. Skip the extra sugar.
- **Cocoa powder:** cheap and handy, but thin on its own. Best mixed with chocolate.
- **Instant hot cocoa mix:** for emergencies. Usually lots of sugar and milk powder.

**Tip:** Dutch-process (alkalized) cocoa is darker, smoother and dissolves especially well.

## 1. Classic hot chocolate (with real chocolate)

**Ingredients (1 large mug):**

- 250 ml (1 cup) milk
- 40 g (1.5 oz) dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cocoa powder
- 1–2 tsp sugar, to taste
- a pinch of salt, a little vanilla if you like

**Method:**

1. Warm the milk in a small pan over medium heat. When small bubbles appear at the edge (about 70 °C / 160 °F), turn the heat down. **Don't boil.**
2. Add the chocolate, cocoa, sugar and salt.
3. **Whisk for 1–2 minutes** until the chocolate has melted and the milk is foamy.
4. Pour into a mug and top with whipped cream or marshmallows.

**Why salt?** A pinch brings out the chocolate flavor and softens the bitterness. You won't taste it.

## 2. Quick cocoa version

No chocolate in the house? Ready in 3 minutes:

1. Put **2 tsp cocoa** and **2 tsp sugar** in the mug.
2. Stir in **2 tbsp hot water** or milk to make a smooth paste. This is the trick against lumps.
3. Pour in **250 ml hot milk** and stir.

For foam, shake the milk in a jar first: [how to froth milk without a frother](/en/how-to-froth-milk-without-a-frother/).

## 3. Thick Italian-style hot chocolate

In Italy, "cioccolata calda" is as thick as pudding and eaten with a spoon.

**Ingredients (2 small cups):**

- 250 ml milk
- 60 g dark chocolate
- 1 tsp **cornstarch**
- 1–2 tsp sugar

**Method:**

1. Mix the cornstarch with a few spoons of **cold** milk in a small bowl.
2. Heat the rest of the milk and melt the chocolate and sugar in it.
3. Stir in the cornstarch milk and cook for **2–3 minutes**, stirring constantly, until it thickens.
4. Serve hot in small cups. It thickens further as it cools.

## 4. With a milk frother

Some electric [milk frothers](/en/milk-frother-guide/) have a hot chocolate setting or a special stirring disc.

1. Pour in the milk (stay under the max line).
2. Add **finely chopped chocolate**, or cocoa and sugar.
3. Start the hot chocolate (or hot foam) program.

⚠️ **Check the manual first.** Not every model can stir chocolate in the jug. If yours can't, heat the milk in the frother and pour it over chocolate melted in the mug.

## Variations

- **Cinnamon:** heat the milk with a cinnamon stick.
- **Orange:** add a strip of orange peel while heating, remove before serving.
- **Mexican-style:** a pinch of chili and cinnamon. Surprisingly good!
- **Hazelnut:** stir in 1 tsp hazelnut butter.
- **Mocha:** add a shot of espresso. More winter recipes: [winter coffee drinks](/en/winter-coffee-drinks/).
- **With syrup:** vanilla, caramel or hazelnut. See our [coffee syrup guide](/en/coffee-syrup-guide/).

## Vegan hot chocolate

It works very well with plant milk. **Oat milk** is the creamiest, **almond milk** lighter. Make sure the chocolate is dairy-free (many dark chocolates are vegan, check the label). More: [oat milk for coffee](/en/oat-milk-for-coffee/).

## Common mistakes

- **Boiling the milk:** a skin forms and it tastes scorched. 65–70 °C (150–160 °F) is plenty.
- **Dumping cocoa into hot milk:** lumps. Make a paste with a little liquid first.
- **Not whisking:** the chocolate sinks. Use a whisk or a frother.
- **Too much sugar:** taste it unsweetened first. Milk chocolate is already sweet.

## FAQ

**Is hot chocolate OK for kids?** Yes. Dark chocolate contains a little caffeine and theobromine, but far less than coffee. For small children, use milk chocolate or less cocoa.

**How long does hot chocolate keep?** **2 days** in a closed container in the fridge. Stir while reheating.

**Can I give it as a gift?** Layer cocoa, sugar, chocolate chips and mini marshmallows in a jar and write the recipe on the label. A lovely winter gift. More ideas: [coffee gift ideas](/en/coffee-gift-ideas/).
