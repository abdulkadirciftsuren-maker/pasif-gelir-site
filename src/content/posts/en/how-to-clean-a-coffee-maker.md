Has your coffee been tasting **bitter**, **sour** or **stale** lately? Often the problem isn't the beans but the **machine**. Coffee oils build up with every brew, go rancid and spoil the flavor, and milk systems can grow bacteria. This guide covers regular cleaning **other than descaling**, **step by step for each type of machine**.

> **Short answer:** **After every use**, dump the grounds and rinse with water. **Every day**, clean the milk system and drip tray. **Once a week**, wash the brew unit, filter basket and water tank. **Once a month**, run a cleaning tablet or clean the grinder. Descaling is a separate job: [how to descale a coffee maker](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/).

## Why clean regularly?

- **Coffee oils go rancid:** Oil left in the machine turns rancid within days and gives coffee a **bitter, metallic** taste.
- **Milk spoils:** Milk left in tubes and frothers starts to go off within hours.
- **Biofilm in the water tank:** A slimy layer forms in water that sits too long.
- **The machine lasts longer:** Clogged holes and dirty gaskets are common causes of breakdowns.

## Cleaning schedule (summary)

| When? | What? |
| --- | --- |
| **After every use** | Dump the grounds, rinse the filter/portafilter, wipe the steam wand |
| **Daily** | Drip tray and grounds bin, milk system, empty the water tank and refill with fresh water |
| **Weekly** | Brew unit, filter basket, wash the water tank, wipe the bean hopper |
| **Monthly** | Cleaning program with a tablet, clean the grinder, check the gaskets |
| **As needed** | Descale (depending on water hardness). See [how to descale a coffee maker](/en/how-to-descale-a-coffee-maker/) |

## By type of machine

### Drip coffee maker

1. **After every use:** Toss the paper filter, rinse the filter basket and carafe.
2. **Weekly:** Wash the carafe and basket in hot soapy water. For brown stains in a glass carafe, a spoonful of **baking soda** with hot water does the trick.
3. **Hot plate:** Wipe it with a damp cloth once it's cool.
4. **Water tank:** Don't leave water sitting in it; use fresh water every day.

More: [drip coffee maker guide](/en/drip-coffee-maker-guide/).

### Super-automatic

1. **Daily:** Empty, rinse and **dry** the **grounds bin** and **drip tray**. Damp grounds go moldy.
2. **Milk system daily:** Run the **milk cleaning program**; take apart and rinse carafe or tube-free systems.
3. **Weekly:** If the **brew unit** is removable, rinse it with **lukewarm water only** and let it dry. **No dish soap**: most manufacturers advise against it because it strips the lubricant.
4. **Monthly (or when prompted):** Run the cleaning program with a **cleaning tablet**.
5. **Water tank:** Wash it weekly and replace the **water filter** on time.

More: [super-automatic espresso machine guide](/en/super-automatic-espresso-machine-guide/).

### Espresso machine

1. **After every shot:** Knock out the puck, rinse the portafilter and flush the group head briefly.
2. **Daily:** Wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth **right after every use** and purge it. Brush the group gasket.
3. **Weekly:** If your machine has a **3-way valve**, **backflush** with a blind basket and cleaner. Soak the baskets in hot water with cleaner.
4. **Monthly:** Unscrew and clean the shower screen.

More: [espresso accessories](/en/espresso-accessories/) and [home espresso machine guide](/en/home-espresso-machine-guide/).

### Capsule machine

1. **Daily:** Empty the used capsule container; capsules left in there go moldy.
2. **Weekly:** Wash the drip tray and capsule container.
3. **Monthly:** Run **plain water** through a few times with no capsule to clean around the needle.

More: [capsule coffee machine guide](/en/capsule-coffee-machine-guide/).

### Moka pot

- Rinse with **hot water only** after each use, no soap; **aluminum** pots discolor in the dishwasher.
- Check the gasket and filter plate now and then; replace a gasket that's gone hard.
- Dry the parts **separately**; don't screw it together wet.

More: [how to use a moka pot](/en/how-to-use-a-moka-pot/).

### French press and AeroPress

- **French press:** Put the grounds in the trash (not down the sink!), take the metal filter apart and wash it; oils collect between the layers.
- **AeroPress:** Push out the puck and rinse the seal. Store the plunger **outside** the chamber so the seal lasts longer.

More: [how to use a French press](/en/how-to-use-a-french-press/) and [how to use an AeroPress](/en/how-to-use-an-aeropress/).

### Turkish coffee machine and cezve

- **Turkish coffee machine:** Rinse and wipe the pot with lukewarm water after every use. Clean the heating base and sensor gently with a soft cloth, **without scratching**. Never put the electric part in water.
- **Cezve:** Wash with hot water and a little soap and dry well. Don't use scouring pads on the tin lining of a copper cezve.

More: [Turkish coffee machine guide](/en/turkish-coffee-machine-guide/).

### Coffee grinder

- **Weekly:** Wipe out the bean hopper (oils build up).
- **Monthly:** Grind **grinder cleaning pellets** or brush out the burrs.
- **Don't grind rice!** It's often suggested, but it can strain the motor and burrs, especially on flat-burr grinders.

More: [best coffee grinder](/en/best-coffee-grinder/).

### Milk frother

- Wash an electric frother **right after each use**; dried milk sticks.
- **Don't scratch** the non-stick coating; use a soft sponge.

More: [milk frother guide](/en/milk-frother-guide/).

## Don'ts

- Don't put a super-automatic's **brew unit** in the dishwasher.
- Don't use **vinegar** for everything; it can damage some gaskets and parts. For descaling, use what the manufacturer recommends.
- Don't pour **grounds down the sink**; over time they clog the drain.
- Don't leave water sitting in the tank for days.

## FAQ

**How often should I clean my coffee maker?** Small jobs daily, the brew unit and water tank weekly, cleaning tablets and the grinder monthly. The schedule above is a good start.

**Why does my coffee taste bitter and stale?** Usually it's **old coffee oil**. Run a cleaning program with a tablet and wash the brew unit and filters; you'll taste the difference right away.

**Is cleaning the same as descaling?** No. Cleaning removes **coffee oils and milk residue**; descaling removes **minerals from the water**. You need both.

**Can I use dish soap instead of cleaning tablets?** No. Dish soap foams, doesn't rinse out well and can damage gaskets. Use tablets made for your machine.
